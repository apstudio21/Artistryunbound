# Setting up your website accounts

Hi! To launch your site we need three accounts that belong to **you**, so you stay in full control. It takes about 15 minutes. Oleksandr will do all the technical parts afterwards.

You only need to: create the accounts, invite Oleksandr, and send him one code.

---

## 1. GitHub (where your site's files and texts live)

1. Go to https://github.com/signup and create a free account. Remember the username and password.
2. After signing in, click **+** (top right) -> **New repository**.
   - Name: `artistry-unbound`
   - Choose **Private**
   - Click **Create repository**
3. Open the new repository -> **Settings** -> **Collaborators** -> **Add people**.
   Type Oleksandr's email: **alexandr.qdq@gmail.com** and send the invite. (He accepts it by email.)

## 2. Cloudflare (what puts your site online)

1. Go to https://dash.cloudflare.com/sign-up and create a free account. Confirm your email.
2. Open **Manage account** -> **Members** -> **Invite**.
   - Email: **alexandr.qdq@gmail.com**
   - Role: **Administrator**
   - Send the invite.

## 3. Telegram bot (sends you a message for every order)

1. In Telegram, search for **@BotFather** (the one with the blue check mark) and open it.
2. Send `/newbot`.
3. Choose a name (for example `Artistry Unbound Orders`), then a username that ends in `bot` (for example `artistry_unbound_orders_bot`).
4. BotFather replies with a long **token** (looks like `123456789:ABC...`).
5. Open your new bot (BotFather gives you a link) and press **Start**. This is important, otherwise the bot cannot message you.
6. Send the token to Oleksandr in a **private message**. Do not post it anywhere public.

---

## 4. Instagram feed (optional, shows your latest posts on the site)
Your Instagram account must be a **Business** or **Creator** account (Instagram app -> Settings -> Account type).
1. Go to https://behold.so and sign up for the free plan.
2. Connect your Instagram account `artistry.unbound` when asked.
3. Create a feed and copy its **JSON feed URL** (it starts with `https://feeds.behold.so/`).
4. Send that link to Oleksandr, or paste it yourself in your editing page under **Settings -> Instagram feed link**.

New posts then appear on the site automatically.

---

Connect the repository to Cloudflare, add your bot, and set up the login for your editing page. Then you will have your own page `/admin` where you can change texts, photos, prices and audio whenever you like, using your GitHub login.
